## Retrieval and handling

- PaperFetcher searches were run for small-molecule antibiotic ML, abaucin, SyntheMol, and prospective generative antibiotic design. PubMed returned the abaucin record; Crossref returned SyntheMol metadata; OpenAlex/Semantic Scholar were rate-limited (HTTP 429) in the targeted runs. PaperFetcher direct `fetch` is arXiv/Hugging Face oriented and returned HTTP 400 for DOI inputs; for the Cell 2025 and 2026 PMC papers, direct PaperFetcher fetch was not a supported path. These errors were not treated as evidence about the science.
- Searches were followed by primary-source reading through publisher pages, PubMed/PMC, a MIT author manuscript for Cell, plus author-hosted published-article PDF copies for the 2023 abaucin and 2024 SyntheMol articles. PaperFetcher metadata/search results were discovery aids, not source authority for claims.
- No matching full-paper PDF was present in `.paper-cache` for the checked identifiers. No full paper, extracted passage, figure, or supplementary file is copied into the repository.
- This checkout does not contain `scripts/claim_audit.py`; therefore no automated `claim_audit` count is reported. The three paper notes instead provide manually checked seven-question evidence maps, with primary-paper figures/pages attached to method, result, comparator and boundary claims. This manual source audit is not a scripted adjacency audit.
- No model code, molecular generation, MIC assay, synthesis, or animal experiment was executed or independently reproduced in this work.
