## Claim decisions in this batch

- Retain `80B total / 13B active per input`. The report explicitly says the shared expert remains active and 8 of 64 non-shared experts are active; it does not define a second `26.5B` active-parameter figure. Removed the community-derived alternative.
- Replace the single-card deployment implication with the pinned repository's BF16 guidance: two GPUs above 80GB each; TP4 recommended for long context. This does not establish a universal minimum for quantized, offloaded, or other backends. The separate HF model card specifies a different 256K BF16 deployment setup (4×H20 96GB); its default config is 32K, and its README requires a manual config change for the full advertised window.
- Removed claims that A13B shares Hunyuan-Large's architecture lineage and serves as a TurboS/T1 backbone; this A13B source set does not establish those relationships.
- Distinguished the repository's 2025-06-26 release/report announcement, the repository PDF cover date 2025-06-26, the arXiv v1 PDF cover date 2025-06-27, and arXiv submission on 2026-09-23.

Cross-family superiority/exclusivity claims remain open. No full-series refresh or independent model reproduction was performed.

## Hunyuan-Large claim decisions (2026-10-22 batch)

- Verified the `nearly 95%` arithmetic from Table 2: for the paper settings, `(2 × n_g)/(4 × n_h) = (2 × 8)/(4 × 80) = 5%` of the MHA BF16 KV tensor bytes. Narrowed the wording to that tensor comparison and baseline.
- Preserved Eq. (2) but defined `N` and `D`, and stated the paper’s scaling-fit range and extrapolated `58.1B / 5.6T` estimate separately from the selected `52B / ~7T` model. Do not treat the equation as a hardware-specific runtime estimate.
- Paper/report source is fixed at arXiv v3; checkpoint/repository identities are recorded separately and were not used to validate benchmark scores.

## HunyuanVideo-1.5 SSTA result correction (2026-10-23 batch)

- Fixed source at arXiv `2511.18870v2` (2025-11-25); checked the official HTML v2. §3.1 describes SSTA as a combination of selected block attention and a local sliding-tile mask.
- §6/Table 7 specifies the CFG-distilled model, 8 NVIDIA H800 GPUs and context parallelism. For 720p, 241-frame T2V, the table reports 5.5070 vs 2.9475 seconds per diffusion step without engineering-level acceleration, corresponding to about 1.87×. Table 8 separately reports the engineering-accelerated 50-step case; those conditions are not mixed with Table 7.
- The fixed paper does not compare HunyuanVideo-1.5 quality to the earlier 13B HunyuanVideo. Removed the “same experience as 13B” framing and bounded SSTA's contribution to the paper's measured setup. No runtime or independent quality comparison was performed.

## Hunyuan-MT WMT25 ranking scope (2026-10-23 batch)

- Checked Hunyuan-MT report arXiv `2509.05209v2` (§4.1–4.3/Table 4) plus the official WMT25 findings paper (`2025.wmt-1.22`, §7.2 and §7.4) and the Shy-hunyuan-MT system paper (`2025.wmt-1.36`, §§1–3).
- The Tencent report states 30/31 first-place language-pair results. The WMT findings paper identifies the entry as `Shy-hunyuan-MT`, says it ranked first in automatic rankings for all but one pair, and cautions that its human-evaluation performance was considerably below top-rated systems. It was the best constrained system, in the constrained winning cluster on 11 directions; Gemini 2.5 Pro was top-cluster overall on 14.
- The system paper describes a task-specific pipeline initialized from Hunyuan-7B with continued pretraining, SFT, RL and candidate fusion. The competition result therefore must not be presented as a direct human-evaluation win for the unmodified Hunyuan-MT-7B checkpoint. The existing Flores/GPT-4.1 parity statement was removed because it collapsed multiple score groups and metrics into one comparison.
